A rank tracker fixes this by running an unbiased query from a clean server, with no personalization, from the country and device you specify. Yes — unlimited manual rank checks with no credit card, no trial, and no rate limits. Saved rank tracking projects are limited to 10 keywords per project on the free plan; paid plans get unlimited keywords per project.


#### H3: How accurate is the rank tracking data?
The rank tracker pulls live, depersonalized search results from a clean IP in the country you select. We do not estimate or extrapolate rankings. Every rank result is a real query the tracker ran when you clicked the button.


#### H3: Can the rank tracker check rankings in any country?
Pick from the supported countries in the location dropdown. The rank tracker queries search engines at the country level, so every check returns the live ranking for the country you select.


#### H3: Does the rank tracker check mobile and desktop rankings separately?
Yes — toggle the Device button before running the rank check. Mobile and desktop rankings often differ by 2–5 positions, so tracking both matters for any keyword with significant mobile search volume.


#### H3: How often should I track my rankings?
For active SEO work, run the rank tracker daily for 5–10 priority keywords and weekly for the rest. Search engine rankings shift daily because of algorithm updates, competitor activity, and content changes. Checking once a month hides what is actually happening to your domain.


#### H3: Can the rank tracker monitor competitor domains?
Yes — enter their domain instead of yours, or use the Competitor Analysis feature to compare their full ranking profile against your own. The rank tracker treats any domain the same — your site or a competitor's.


#### H3: Why is my domain ranking lower than I expected?
The most common reasons are weak internal linking to the page, thin content compared to top-ranking pages, slow page speed, or mismatched search intent. Run the AI Recommendation Plan after a rank check — it diagnoses which of these is hurting your domain ranking most.

We store the keywords and domains you save to rank tracking projects. We do not sell data, run ads against your usage, or share with third parties. Anonymous one-off rank checks are not logged to your account.
