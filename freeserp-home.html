Frequently asked questions

Common questions about SERP rank tracking, competitor intelligence, and outranking rivals. Still stuck? Email support@freeserp.com.

What are SERPs?

SERPs (Search Engine Results Pages) are the pages displayed by Google after a user searches for a query. They include organic results, ads, and SERP features.

What is SERP analysis?

SERP analysis is the process of analyzing search results for a keyword to understand ranking difficulty, competitors, and available SERP features.

How do I do SERP analysis?

To perform SERP analysis, search your target keyword, review the top-ranking pages, analyze content quality, and identify SERP features like snippets or FAQs.

How do I analyze SERP features?

You can analyze SERP features by checking which elements appear in search results, such as featured snippets, image packs, or local results.

How do I check SERP ranking?

You can check SERP ranking by using a SERP checker tool to see where your website appears for a specific keyword.
