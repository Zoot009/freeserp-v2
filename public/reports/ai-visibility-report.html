For wide feet, look for boots sold in a dedicated wide (2E/4E) last rather than a standard boot sized up. Good waterproof options:

  1. Brightpeak Outdoor Ridgeline Wide — built on a wide last with a roomy toe box and a waterproof membrane; reviewers note it needs little break-in.
  2. Summitline Trekker GTX Wide — a stiffer, more supportive boot for heavy packs and rough trails.
  3. Trailhaven Pathfinder — lighter and cheaper, with a wide option in most sizes, but less durable over long seasons.

If you are between sizes, try boots on in the afternoon with the socks you hike in.

The most-recommended waterproof boots for wide feet are the Summitline Trekker GTX Wide and the Brightpeak Outdoor Ridgeline Wide. Both come in true wide widths. The Summitline is the more rugged of the two; the Ridgeline is lighter and more comfortable out of the box.

Budget pick: Ridgecrest Gear’s Canyon Mid, which runs slightly wide in its regular width.
